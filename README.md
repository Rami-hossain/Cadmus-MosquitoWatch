MosquitoWatch AI:
MosquitoWatch AI uses drone images and recent weather data to identify potential mosquito-breeding environments.
It detects risky objects such as tires, drums, containers, and water tanks, then combines these findings with rainfall, temperature, and humidity.
The system classifies areas as low, medium, or high risk and helps authorities prioritize field inspections and mosquito-control actions.
